About Sipster

Sipster is a native desktop SIP client written in Rust. It lets you register with your VoIP provider or local PBX (such as an AVM Fritz!Box or any standard SIP registrar) to place and receive high-definition phone calls straight from your computer.

Designed for speed, low resource usage, and privacy, Sipster connects directly to your SIP servers and synchronizes contacts from sources you configure. It does not transmit your personal data, audio, or credentials to any third-party intermediary servers.

📞 Modern SIP Calling

Full support for inbound and outbound calls, two-way audio with PipeWire / ALSA / WASAPI device selection, early media, DTMF (RFC 4733), hold, call transfer, and custom ringtones.

👥 Integrated Contacts

Sync contacts seamlessly across multiple sources including Google Contacts, FRITZ!Box phonebook, CardDAV servers, Evolution Data Server, KDE Akonadi, and local vCard directories.

🔒 Local & Private

All authentication tokens, SIP passwords, call logs, and synced contacts are stored strictly on your local machine. No external tracking, telemetry, or third-party servers.

Google Contacts Integration & Data Usage

Sipster includes optional support for importing your Google Contacts. When you choose to connect your Google account:

  • OAuth Authentication: Sipster uses Google's secure OAuth 2.0 flow to request read-only access (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/contacts.readonly) to your contacts.
  • Local Processing: Contact names and phone numbers are downloaded directly to your local computer to display in the dialer address book and resolve caller IDs.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: Your Google user data is never uploaded, transferred, or sold to any external servers or third parties.

For complete information on how your data is handled, please review our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
